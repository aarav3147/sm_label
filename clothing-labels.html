Woven Labels

The benchmark in apparel branding. High-density polyester and cotton damask weaves engineered with laser-sealed edges, center folds, and end folds.

  • High-definition damask & taffeta weaves
  • Pantone thread color matching
  • Non-fraying ultrasonic soft borders

Satin Labels

Smooth, skin-friendly satin labels delivering a silky sheen. Ideal for evening gowns, lingerie, baselayers, and multi-page care instruction ribbons.

  • Silky touch with zero skin irritation
  • Woven or printed satin options
  • Excellent wash fastness

Printed Labels

Clear, sharp printing on cotton tape, satin ribbons, and nylon taffeta. Perfect for detailed wash symbols, composition percentages, and legal compliance.

  • Multi-color rotary & screen printing
  • Crisp micro-typography down to 1mm
  • Resistant to industrial washing detergents

PU Labels

Modern polyurethane faux leather badges designed for denim, casual bottoms, hoodies, and jackets. Available with debossed, embossed, or printed branding.

  • Animal-friendly synthetic leather
  • Machine washable with denim enzyme washes
  • Deep 3D debossed branding relief

Leather Labels & Patches

Heavyweight leather patches for jeans back-waistbands, workwear, caps, and outerwear. Features hot-stamp debossing, perimeter stitch grooves, and vintage tanning.
