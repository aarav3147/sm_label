Ghaziabad Facility FAQs

Where is SM Labels located in Ghaziabad? expand_more

Our primary North India manufacturing facility and corporate office is located in Mohan Nagar, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, conveniently connected to East Delhi, Noida Sector 62/63, and the Grand Trunk Road apparel manufacturing corridors.

Can apparel manufacturers in Noida and Delhi visit for physical sampling? expand_more

Yes, garment merchandisers, fashion designers, and factory production heads are welcome to schedule an in-person sampling session at our Mohan Nagar unit to inspect thread shade cards, weave densities, and foil-stamped hang tags.

What is the delivery turnaround time for garment exporters in Delhi NCR? expand_more

Standard bulk orders are delivered within 4 to 7 business days across Noida, Greater Noida, Okhla, Gurugram, and Delhi, with rapid local courier dispatch directly from our Mohan Nagar facility.
