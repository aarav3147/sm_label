Answers for Apparel Brands

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding custom clothing label manufacturing, minimums, sampling, and delivery.

What is the difference between damask woven labels and satin labels?

Damask woven labels use high-density, fine-denier polyester yarns interlaced on jacquard looms to create textured, long-lasting brand graphics. Satin labels feature a lustrous, silky sheen with a smoother, softer hand feel, making them ideal for luxury eveningwear, baby garments, and wash care instruction tags.

Can you match custom Pantone brand colors for woven threads?

Yes. We calibrate yarn selection directly to the Pantone Matching System (PMS). Our facilities maintain an extensive palette of stock dyed yarns, and custom dye lots are available for high-volume brand requirements.

Where are your production facilities located?

SM Labels operates two dedicated manufacturing units: Unit 1 in Mohan Nagar, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh (serving Delhi NCR, Noida, Gurgaon, and Northern India) and Unit 2 on Naroda Road, Ahmedabad, Gujarat (serving Surat, Ahmedabad, Mumbai, and Western India). Both units maintain pan-India express shipping.
