What Are Printed Clothing Labels?

Printed clothing labels are created by applying wash-resistant pigment inks via rotary flexographic, screen, or thermal transfer printing directly onto pre-woven ribbons. They are indispensable for displaying high-density legal information, multi-language washing symbols, barcodes, and fiber content that would be impossible to weave legibly.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What information is mandatory on a garment care label?

Garment regulations generally require fiber composition percentages, standardized wash care symbols (washing, bleaching, drying, ironing, dry cleaning), country of origin, and registered manufacturer/brand identification.

Can printed labels be cut into individual tabs or supplied in rolls?

We can deliver labels pre-cut and folded (center-fold or straight cut with sealed ends) ready for immediate stitching, or supplied in continuous rolls for automated factory cutting stations.
