FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between leather labels and PU labels?

Leather labels offer an organic, heavyweight tactile presence with natural grain variations and tonal heat burnishing. PU labels are made from synthetic polyurethane, offering a cruelty-free vegan alternative that is exceptionally uniform and machine washable.

Can you dye leather patches to match custom brand colors?

We provide classic heritage leather tones including tan, dark brown, cognac, saddle, and matte black, and we can match custom dyed leather shades for large volume manufacturing orders.
