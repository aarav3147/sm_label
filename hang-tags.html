FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can hang tags include variable retail barcodes and price stickers?

Yes. We can print scannable EAN/UPC barcodes, QR codes, style numbers, and MRP directly onto the tags or provide synchronized thermal barcode sticker sets.

Do you supply string cords and security seal locks with hang tags?

Yes. We supply hang tags pre-punched with reinforced eyelets and offer waxed cotton cords, satin ribbons, elastic loops, and tamper-evident molded plastic seal cubes with your embossed logo.
