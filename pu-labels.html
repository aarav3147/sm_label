Why Apparel Brands Choose Custom PU Labels

Polyurethane (PU) leather badges impart an authentic, rugged, or sleek industrial signature to garments. Unlike traditional animal leather, modern PU substrates can be engineered in virtually any color, grain texture, and thickness while easily enduring harsh commercial denim laundries.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can PU labels be customized in colors other than brown and black?

Yes. We stock PU substrates in classic tan, saddle brown, cognac, white, navy, olive, grey, and black, and custom color matching is available for volume runs.

How are PU labels attached to garments?

Most PU patches feature perimeter stitching channels for sewing directly onto garments. We can also provide heat-seal thermo-adhesive backing for heat press application.
