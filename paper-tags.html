Technical Specifications for Paper Tags

Substrate Options Brown Kraft Paper, Bleached White Kraft, Black Recycled Board, Handmade Textured Cotton Paper, FSC Certified Uncoated Stock
Paper Grammage (GSM) 250 GSM, 300 GSM, 350 GSM, 400 GSM, up to 450 GSM single ply (or duplex pasted for greater thickness)
Printing Methods High-opacity Screen Printing (crisp white on brown kraft), Offset CMYK, Letterpress Deep Deboss, Metallic Foil Stamping
Die Cutting & Punching Standard 3mm or 5mm drill hole, custom geometric die cuts, rounded corners, tear-off perforation lines for price stub
String Attachments Natural unbleached twisted cotton, raw jute rope, waxed flax string, safety pin loops, kraft paper seal locks

Frequently Asked Questions

Paper Tags Manufacturing FAQs

What eco-friendly paper stocks are available for clothing tags? expand_more

We offer virgin brown and bleached white kraft paper, 100% post-consumer recycled paper, handmade seed paper, and FSC-certified uncoated textured art cards ranging from 280 GSM to 450 GSM.

Can paper tags be printed with white ink on brown kraft card? expand_more

Yes. We utilize high-opacity screen printing and specialized UV white ink to ensure crisp, vibrant white typography and branding directly onto earthy brown kraft stock.

What types of natural string attachments do you supply? expand_more

For organic apparel collections, we provide raw jute twine, unbleached natural cotton cords, wax-coated braided threads, and compostable paper strings paired with metal-free punch holes.
