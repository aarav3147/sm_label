FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can satin labels have full-color printing?

Yes. We produce high-resolution rotary and thermal printed satin labels capable of rendering multi-color brand logos, Pantone shades, and fine wash symbol icons.

Do satin edges fray over time?

Our satin ribbon labels feature woven selvedge edges along the top and bottom borders and ultrasonic heat sealed end-cuts, guaranteeing they do not unravel during laundering.
